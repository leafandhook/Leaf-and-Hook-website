About Bechara & Leaf & Hook
Ten Years Of Practice.
A Lifetime In Nature.

Breathwork facilitator, cold and thermal guide, multi-instrumentalist, and internationally certified practitioner, Bechara El Khoury turned what nature, method, and committed practice taught him into a structured practice, experience, and journey for thousands of people ready to come back to themselves. Everything he teaches, he's lived first.

The Leaf represents growth and renewal, inspired by the natural world. The Hook reflects the strength of meaningful experiences and the connections that shape us, the moments that catch you off guard and hold on long after they've passed. Together, Leaf & Hook is a practice built from breath, cold, sound, and nature, and one promise underneath all of it: come back to yourself.
